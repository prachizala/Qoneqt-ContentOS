## 2. Interview Q&A Cheatsheet

### Q: Did you write the core multi-agent video rendering engine in Qoneqt?
> **Answer:** "No, Qoneqt ContentOS was an upstream collaborative project / fork where my primary role was project structuring, licensing, and authoring the architecture presentation deck (`PPT.html`). For my original software engineering work, I invite you to evaluate my **AI Smart Attendance System** (FastAPI, OpenCV, React Native), my **Workforce Attendance Data Analytics** case study, or **MathSprint**."

### Q: Why is this important to state?
> **Honesty Principle:** Recruiters and technical interviewers respect developers who are completely transparent about their individual contributions versus team/forked code. Claiming code you didn't write leads to rejection in deep-dive technical rounds.
